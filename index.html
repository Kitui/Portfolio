02 ABOUT ME

From technology delivery to building dependable data and intelligent systems.

PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

A connected profile across data engineering, intelligent systems and technical delivery.

I am a data engineering, applied AI and technical delivery professional with more than seven years of experience across technology implementation, product development, performance analytics and cross-functional project delivery.

My background gives me a practical understanding of both sides of a technical initiative: the organisational problem that needs to be solved and the engineering required to turn data into a dependable, usable solution.

I work across the complete solution lifecycle—from requirements and architecture to ingestion, transformation, modelling, validation, deployment, governance, monitoring and analytical communication. Depending on the problem, these solutions may run in cloud, lakehouse, distributed or conventional data environments.
